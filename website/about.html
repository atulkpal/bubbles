About Bubbles

Bubbles is a casual bubble popping game built for quick sessions and calm focus. Clear bubbles before the timer runs out, chain combos, avoid bombs, and unlock power-ups as each level gets faster.

Game Modes

Adventure

Structured Stages

10 levels with increasing challenge, boss battles, and environmental hazards.

Zen

Relaxed Play

Pop bubbles without any time pressure. Pure calm.

Daily

Daily Challenge

A fresh timed run to revisit each day. Earn bonus coins.

Power-ups

  • Slow Motion — Slows down the timer for easier combos
  • Freeze — Pauses all bubbles briefly
  • Multi-Pop — Pops multiple bubbles at once in a radius
  • Prism Boost — Enhances special chain effects
