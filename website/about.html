About Hushpop

Hushpop (formerly Bubbles) is a calm soap-bubble game for short breaks. Pop real-looking bubbles, blow your own, breathe along with one, and collect soaps as you go. When you want a goal, there are 20 Challenge levels.

Game Modes

Challenge

When you want a goal

20 timed Challenge levels with chains and boss bubbles, never under 30 seconds.

Float

Calm by default

Soap bubbles rise and drift away. No timer, no fail. Hold anywhere to blow your own, or start a guided Breathe session.

Soaps

Soap of the Day

A different soap each day, the same for everyone, free to use that day.

Challenge power-ups

  • Slow Motion — Bubbles drift at a quarter of their speed for a few seconds
  • Freeze — Holds every bubble still for a moment
  • Multi-Pop — Pops every bubble near your tap
