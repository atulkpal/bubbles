Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Introduction

Ashwath AI ("we", "our", or "us") makes Hushpop (formerly "Bubbles"; the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, by whom, and the choices you have.

In short: we run no servers, no accounts and no analytics of our own. Your progress (pops, soaps, settings) stays on your device. The only data leaving your device goes to our ad partner (Unity LevelPlay) to show ads, and to Google Play if you buy the Supporter Pack.

Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

When ads are shown, our advertising partner may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, IP address)
  • Your device's Advertising ID
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, watched or tapped)

Gameplay information (pops, soaps unlocked, settings, achievements) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it.

Information We Do Not Collect: We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Payments for the Supporter Pack are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details.

How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and improve the App's functionality
  • Display advertisements through our ad partner (and limit how often they appear)
  • Confirm a Supporter Pack purchase so ads are removed
  • Ensure the security and proper functioning of the App

Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) — Our mediation platform for displaying advertisements. Unity Ads and ironSource Exchange may collect your Advertising ID, device information, and usage data for ad serving and personalization. Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services — For Advertising ID access.
  • Google Play Billing — Processes the optional Supporter Pack purchase. We receive only a purchase confirmation. Google Privacy Policy

These third-party services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties except as described in this Privacy Policy. We may share information with:

  • Third-party advertising partners (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource) who receive your Advertising ID for ad serving purposes
  • Third-party service providers who assist in operating the App
  • Law enforcement or government agencies when required by law

Your Choices

  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • The Supporter Pack removes all ads from the App.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all game data stored on your device.

Data Retention

We do not keep any of your data on our own systems. Game progress is stored locally on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App. Data collected by our ad partner is retained under its own policy.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
