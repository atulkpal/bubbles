Your Data, Your Control

Bubbles is designed with minimal data collection. Here's how your data is handled.

Game Progress & Local Data

All game progress, scores, coins, and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data on our servers.

To delete your local game data:

  1. Go to your device's Settings
  2. Navigate to Apps → Bubbles
  3. Tap Storage → Clear Data
  4. Alternatively, simply uninstall the app to remove all local data

Advertising Data

Third-party advertising services (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource) may collect anonymous usage data for ad personalization. To reset your advertising ID:

  • Android: Go to Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  • You can also opt out of personalized ads in the same menu

Request Data Deletion

If you have specific data deletion requests beyond what's described above, you can contact us:

Email: ashwathai.dev@gmail.com

We will respond to your request within 30 days.

What We Don't Have

Since we do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number), there is no personal data stored on our servers to delete. All game data remains on your device under your control.
