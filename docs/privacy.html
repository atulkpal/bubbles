Introduction

Bubbles ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application Bubbles (the "App").

Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App, we may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers)
  • Usage data (gameplay statistics, session duration, in-game actions)
  • Advertising identifiers

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not collect precise geolocation data
  • We do not collect financial or payment information

How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and improve the App's functionality
  • Display relevant advertisements through third-party ad networks
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Ensure the security and proper functioning of the App

Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google AdMob / LevelPlay (IronSource) - For displaying advertisements. These services may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization.
  • Google Play Services - For app functionality and analytics.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties except as described in this Privacy Policy. We may share information with:

  • Third-party service providers who assist in operating the App
  • Law enforcement or government agencies when required by law

Data Retention

We retain automatically collected information for as long as you use the App and for a reasonable period thereafter. Game progress data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the App.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
